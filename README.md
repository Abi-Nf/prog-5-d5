# Coffee machine

A simulation of how a coffee machine and the whole stuffs work in a coffee store

## The business

- The customers pay for a coffee
- Then, They choose one variety of coffee available
- Get finally a coffee

## Use cases

- When customers wanna drink coffee, they pay to an automated machine then choose one variety (maybe a perfume). They wait and get the cup of coffee

## Possible errors

- the given money is not enough to get a drink
- no more coffee capsule/variety
- lack of resource like water and/or sugar
- overstocking the machine with the resources
- something's broke with the machine
- temperature overheat due to some problems
- current cut and impossible to serve a coffee at time
- a cup got stuck in the dispensing process
- no more cup
